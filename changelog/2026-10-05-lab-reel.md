# Lab reel for the TV (/reel)

A looping tour of the lab for the TV in TEC 340: a 3D model of the room, a
flight down into a photo of it, one poster per line of work opening into an
animated scene of that paper's result, then the people and the address.

- New route `/reel` (noindex, not in the nav, filtered out of the sitemap).
  It runs script, so AGENTS.md now lists it as the third exception to the
  no-JavaScript rule, under the same condition: without script it is a stack
  of 16:9 frames that reads like the deck.
- New npm dependencies: `three` (the 3D room) and `gsap` (the timeline), both
  bundled at build time. The reel's script is about 970 KB, 300 KB gzipped;
  no other page loads it.
- The room is rebuilt from a new photo of the lab (`public/reel/room.webp`,
  1024x768), which shows the real AIMS LAB sign, the couch and the posters.
  The calibration (focal length, camera turn and height, room size) is
  measured off the photo and written at the top of `src/scripts/reel/lab.ts`.
  The homepage banner (`public/lab.webp`) is unchanged.
- The opening overhead model sits on a paper backdrop with ambient occlusion,
  block walls, the striped carpet tiles, the couch and ottomans.
- Posters hang over the five real posters in the photo, plus two on bare wall.
  LAPSE has no project page, so its poster uses the paper's Figure 1
  (`public/reel/lapse-overview.webp`, from the figures pipeline).
- Numbers in the scenes come from the SEAM and TIAP demo data or from
  `src/data/reel.ts`, which cites the arXiv abstract for each.
