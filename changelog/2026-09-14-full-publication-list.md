# Full publication list

`src/data/publications.ts` held only the six current papers. It now carries the
vision and UAV back catalogue, 11 more entries running from 2025 back to 2020.

The earlier network-on-chip papers published under the name Rabab Ezz-Eldin
were prepared and then held back at Rabab's request. They are not in the file;
the DOIs are recorded in this entry's history if they are wanted later.

Every link was resolved against Semantic Scholar's record for the three author
IDs rather than retyped from a CV, then checked against doi.org. Preference
order for `url` was DOI, then arXiv where no DOI exists.

Notes on specific entries:

- The ICCV 2023 CLIP paper is listed under its published title, `CDUL: ...`,
  and as ICCV, not CVPR. The source list said "CVPR (ICCV)"; ICCV is correct.
- G2Net (BMVC 2022) has no working proceedings URL. `bmvc2022.mpi-inf.mpg.de`
  redirects to `papers.bmvc2022.org`, which 404s for this paper. The entry
  points at the arXiv version, 2210.11469, which is titled G2NetPL. Swap it for
  the proceedings page if that host comes back.
- PLMCL (ECCV Workshops 2022) points at arXiv 2208.09999; no Springer DOI was
  found for the workshop version.

Ordering follows the existing convention, so the homepage "Recent" three are
unchanged: 2026 published work, then the paper under revision, then the older
published work, then the 2026 arXiv preprints.
