# UR2PhD page, a lead project, and matching page styles

- New page `/ur2phd` for the CRA Undergraduate Research to PhD mentoring
  program, which Dr. Abdelfattah coordinates at USM. Copy follows the School
  of Computing Sciences and Computer Engineering flyer, in the site's charcoal
  and gold: goals band, how-it-works steps, audience cards, funding tiles,
  sessions, how to apply (mailto with subject `UR2PhD_YourName`), and the
  Summer 2026 cohort from `Summer 2026 UR2PhD.xlsx` (23 names) in
  `src/data/ur2phd.ts`. The nav link to News became UR2PhD; `/news` is still
  linked from the homepage news panel.
- A lead-project homepage layout (one full-width cover, two below) and a
  two-column `/projects` were tried and reverted in the same session; the
  grid, featured order, and Gaussian thumb are as they were.
- Project cards and project pages use the site's card and pill styles.
- Homepage: a charcoal "Join the lab" card under the research tags balances
  the news panel. Section titles match the other pages (36px, blue and gold
  bar), and that bar is now the same on every page that has one.
- `/what-is-aims`: text beside icon panels (topics, student skills), and the
  research-loop figure on an off-white panel with a gold centre and icon
  discs.
- `/people`: Ticauris Stokes is both a current PhD student and a Spring 2026
  master's alum.
