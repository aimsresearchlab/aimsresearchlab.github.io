## Overview

Power lines, surface cracks, and retinal vessels are studied by separate
communities but pose the same problem: the target occupies a handful of pixels
per row, so class imbalance and connectivity dominate everything else.

## Scope

The survey traces these three domains together, from classical filtering and
hand-designed pipelines through deep segmentation networks to promptable
foundation models, and reads across them for the methods that transfer.
